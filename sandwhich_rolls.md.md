**Makes Six Rolls**

**Tags:** vegetarian

A very wet, no-knead dough that goes from bowl to oven in under two hours. There's no shaping — you just dump the risen dough onto a floured counter and cut it into six squares. The salt is kept to 3/4 teaspoon, a cut small enough that the rolls still taste fully seasoned.

### Ingredients:

- 4 cups all-purpose flour, plus extra for the counter
- 2 teaspoons instant yeast
- 1 teaspoon honey
- 1 teaspoon fine salt
- 2 cups warm water
- 1 tablespoon olive oil

Stir the honey into the warm water until it dissolves. In a large bowl, combine the flour, yeast, and salt. Add the honey water and olive oil, and mix with a spatula until no dry flour remains.

With wet hands, pull one edge of the dough up and fold it over onto itself. Turn the bowl 45 degrees and repeat, for eight folds total.

Cover the bowl with plastic wrap and set it in a warm spot to rise for 1 hour.

Heavily flour the counter and turn the risen dough out onto it. Flour the top, then cut it into six roughly equal pieces with a knife or bench scraper. Move the pieces to a parchment-lined baking sheet.

Bake at 440F for about 30 minutes, until golden brown.

## Allergens

- **Gluten / wheat** — all-purpose flour.

These rolls are vegetarian. The honey means they are not vegan; use 1/2 teaspoon of sugar instead for a vegan version, with no change to the rise or flavor. They are free of egg, dairy, nuts, soy, shellfish, and fish.

**Nutritional note:** Fairly liver-friendly — very low in saturated fat, and the only added fat is a tablespoon of olive oil. The salt is kept to 3/4 teaspoon (about 290 mg sodium per roll). Going below 3/4 teaspoon starts to make the rolls taste flat and the dough rise too fast, so trim further only if sodium matters more than flavor. These are refined-flour rolls, so swapping up to a cup of the flour for whole wheat adds fiber.
